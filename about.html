About AI Disaster Rescue Planner

A student prototype demonstrating how disaster information can be organised to support planning discussions.

Problem statement

During disasters, information about affected communities, road accessibility, and available resources can be fragmented. This portal concept brings scenario assessment, historical case studies, route options, and resource summaries into one interface.

Objective

Present disaster-related information in a clear, structured dashboard.

Current implementation

Multi-page website with JavaScript-based demonstration logic and illustrative data.

Future enhancement

Add verified data sources, a validated machine-learning model, and real road-network services with safety controls.

System workflow

Scenario input→Data checks→Demo risk scoring→Recommendations

Current risk scoring is rule-based, not a trained AI model.

Safety notice

Educational prototype using illustrative demo data. Not connected to live emergency services, GPS, official alerts, or verified road conditions. Never use these suggestions in a real emergency.
